04 · Your visit

What a first visit looks like.

No surprises between the phone call and the cup of water at the end.

  1. Before

    Reserve a time

    Call or WhatsApp your nearest branch with the treatment, day and number of guests.

  2. Arrival

    Settle in

    Arrive a little early. Mention anything your therapist should know, and the pressure you like.

  3. The hour

    Your treatment

    Oil-based massages can leave a trace on clothing, so wear something comfortable.

  4. After

    Take it slowly

    Give yourself a few unhurried minutes before heading back out into Nagpur traffic.
