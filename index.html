06First visit

What to expect, start to finish.

  1. Book ahead

    Call or message the branch you prefer with the treatment, the day and how many of you are coming.

  2. Arrive a little early

    Settle in at the lounge. Tell your therapist how much pressure you like and anything they should know.

  3. Your treatment

    Most massages use oil, so wear clothes you won’t mind getting a little oil on.

  4. Afterwards

    Take a few unhurried minutes before heading back out into Nagpur.
